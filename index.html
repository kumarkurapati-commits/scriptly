How to use Scriptly

Everything you need to know to write your first screenplay.
1

Sign in or use free

Sign in with Google or email/password to save unlimited scripts to the cloud. Use it free to write one script without an account.

2

Start writing

Click Write a script then + New script. Or pick a template for a pre-built structure.

3

Scene headings

The editor starts on Scene heading. Type where your scene takes place:

INT. COFFEE SHOP — DAY
4

Block types

Click a pill to switch type. Enter to auto-advance. Tab to cycle types. Character names autocomplete.

5

Auto-save & history

Saves every 30 seconds automatically. Click 🕐 History to see and restore older versions.

6

Format checker

Click ✅ Check to scan your script for formatting issues.

7

Learn filmmaking

126 courses on directors, writing craft, and technique. Progress saved permanently. Earn certificates by completing quizzes.

8

Writing streaks & stats

Your daily writing streak, word counts, and activity heatmap are in 📊 Stats. Set a daily goal to stay motivated.
